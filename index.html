About Me

Since a very young age, video games were a major interest to me. As I grew up, I found that coding was a very fun challenge and was something I could strive for. At first, I went to the University of Toledo, majoring in Computer Science, because I thought that I wouldn't have much of a shot in game development. After a year there, I decided to transfer to Ohio University to major in VR and Game Development. By transferring, I have confirmed that my passion is game development. Now, I am graduating in the Fall 2026 semester and am looking for internships for the Summer 2026 semester. While gaining much coding knowledge in college, I also have experience programming at Protec Coating Company, Lantern Light Studios, and for Ohio University on a grant project. I am extremely dedicated and eager to find new projects and work that I can challenge myself with, and bring forth value to my team.
