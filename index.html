Projects

BattleLine

BattleLine was a result of Lantern Light Studios' first ever PADD event (month long game jam). I acted as the lead designer and programmer of the project. I took inspiration from games like Chess and Clash Royale, but wanted to make a simultaneous turn based game. The game is fun because of the medieval aesthetic and unique choices that the player can make. From the start of the game, the player is able to choose their lineup of characters, as well as starting positions. This project helped me with team leadership, communication, my programming skills, and my overall understanding of game design. I also was able to market the game to potential players, which allowed me to network with players and receive constructive feedback on the game's design and mechanics.

  • Lead Designer
  • Programming multiplayer systems
  • Marketing the game to players
  • Leading and organizing tasks for my team

Simoné

Simoné is a memory game where players attempt to remember a sequence of colors, while strategically choosing upgrades to gain points and climb the leaderboard. The aesthetic of Simoné is heavily based on the French Quarter in New Orleans. As the player begins the game, they will be met with the smooth, yet degrading voice of our radio host, Simoné. Players will have to be focused and determined to get past her and remember the sequence.

  • Programming in Unity C#
  • Game Design
  • Producer

Me, Myself, and I

Me, Myself, and I is a 2D platformer where players must die to pass the level. The main character, Eugene, finds himself stuck in a large scientific lab, where he has clearly been experimented on. His goal is to make it out of this lab by using clones of himself to block, circumvent, or outright sacrifice to clear a path through the weapons in the lab.

  • Programming character controller, death interactions
  • Playtesting character controls to enhance gameplay
