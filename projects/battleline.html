BattleLine

BattleLine was a result of Lantern Light Studios' first ever PADD event (month long game jam). I acted as the lead designer and programmer of the project. I took inspiration from games like Chess and Clash Royale, but wanted to make a simultaneous turn based game. The game is fun because of the medieval aesthetic and unique choices that the player can make. From the start of the game, the player is able to choose their lineup of characters, as well as starting positions. This project helped me with team leadership, communication, my programming skills, and my overall understanding of game design. I also was able to market the game to potential players, which allowed me to network with players and receive constructive feedback on the game's design and mechanics.

  • Lead Designer
  • Programming multiplayer systems
  • Marketing the game to players
  • Leading and organizing tasks for my team
