RedHouse VR

Description:

RedHouse VR is a simulation training built for the Ohio Peace Officer Training Academy (OPOTA). It is funded by the State of Ohio's Attorney General's Office. My main duties included testing the simulation for bugs and potential feature improvements. I helped hold playtests for the general public during the GRID Lab's 20th Anniversary and a few for OPOTA members. Most of my programming for this project was to fix bugs and work on doors and drawers. This project gave me a grand opportunity to interact with the Meta XR SDK and to better understand VR development. It also allowed me to gain experience in showing off the simulation and explaining the ins and outs of the training.

My Role:

  • Programmed with the Meta XR SDK in Unity
  • Assisted in holding playtests for OPOTA trainees
  • Playtested to discover bugs and potential improvements
