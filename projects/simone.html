Simoné

Description:

Simoné is a memory game where players attempt to remember a sequence of colors, while strategically choosing upgrades to gain points and climb the leaderboard. The aesthetic of Simoné is heavily based on the French Quarter in New Orleans. As the player begins the game, they will be met with the smooth, yet degrading voice of our radio host, Simoné. Players will have to be focused and determined to get past her and remember the sequence.

My Role:

  • Programming in Unity C#
  • Game Design
  • Producer
