PORTS Sim

Description:

The PORTS Sim was made to teach about the deconstruction plans for the PORTS Gaseous Diffusion Plant in Piketon, Ohio to the general public. I was brought onto the project near the end of its development, where most of my work revolved around patching and bug fixing. I worked under the Voinovich School of Leadership and Public Service, as well as the Department of Energy for this project, providing solutions to requested changes and updates to the model.

My Role:

  • Revised changes requested by the Department of Energy
  • Playtested the simulation for bugs and potential improvements
